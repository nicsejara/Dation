
# 4. PARTIAL COVERAGE

Un plan parcial puede ser un resultado válido.

Ejemplo:

```text
demand 70
capacity 52
→ assigned 52
→ residual 18
→ CAPACITY_GAP
```

No convertirlo en technical failure.

Debe distinguirse:

- mathematical feasibility;
- policy feasibility;
- full coverage;
- partial coverage;
- hard constraint status.
